## Brand & Style

This design system crafts a pristine, reliable, and commercially efficient interface tailored for modern agribusiness and direct farm-to-table commerce. It bridges pastoral authenticity with clinical modern cleanliness, evoking confidence, freshness, and frictionless trade.

The design philosophy unites:
- **Corporate Agribusiness Minimalism:** A disciplined 60-30-10 distribution utilizing stark white airiness (`#FFFFFF`), authoritative deep navy blue (`#1E3A5F`), and vibrant solar yellow accents (`#FFD700`).
- **Tactile Commerce Cues:** Dedicated high-conversion triggers driven by conversational utility (`#25D366`), inviting immediate customer engagement.
- **Micro-elevation & Physicality:** Tactile cards, subtle ambient depth, and controlled organic transitions (sweeping curved masks) that break geometric monotony while maintaining operational clarity.

Copy and visual weight lean into radical economy—eschewing dense narrative paragraphs in favor of bold typographic anchors, high-fidelity imagery, badge-driven metrics, and direct transactional touchpoints.

## Colors

The palette is governed strictly by the 60-30-10 color rule to establish predictable visual balance and decisive transactional funnels:

- **60% Dominant (Canvas & Air):** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) forms the foundational surface, punctuated by Light Neutral (`#F8FAFC`) to differentiate structural container modules without inducing visual fatigue.
- **30% Secondary (Anchor & Authority):** Deep Navy Blue (`#1E3A5F`) commands structural containers (Navbar, Footer, sticky headers), major typographic hierarchies, and standard button variants. It projects established corporate reliability.
- **10% Accent (Energy & Conversion):** Vibrant Yellow (`#FFD700`) is reserved for high-visibility highlights, flash badges, inventory alerts, and primary hero action anchors.
- **Utility Commerce (Direct Channel):** WhatsApp Green (`#25D366`) is treated as a dedicated semantic transactional token, reserved strictly for instant direct-ordering pathways and conversational actions.
- **Notification Contrast:** Notification indicators leverage Alert Red (`#EF4444`) strictly for micro-badges and chat prompts.

## Typography

Typography is powered by `Inter` across all text tiers, deploying strict optical hierarchy and weight differentiation rather than font cross-pairing. 

- **Display & Headings:** Rendered in Bold (`700`) and Extra Bold (`800`) with negative letter tracking to guarantee crisp readability against deep backgrounds and large hero imagery.
- **Labels & Interactive Triggers:** Rendered in Medium (`500`) and Semi-Bold (`600`) to sustain visual density across buttons, pills, chips, and stock indicators.
- **Copy Restraint:** Copy density must be kept sparse. Avoid multi-paragraph narratives; headings, key metrics, and succinct single-sentence captions take precedence.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth combines clean surface separation with soft, wide-dispersion ambient drop shadows. Surfaces must never feel harsh, dirty, or muddy:

- **Level 0 (Flat):** Base canvas `#FFFFFF` and alternating panel `#F8FAFC`. Zero elevation.
- **Level 1 (Subtle Rest):** Sticky Navigation and resting cards: `box-shadow: 0 4px 6px -1px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.06), 0 2px 4px -2px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.04)`.
- **Level 2 (Active Hover & Modals):** Interactive card elevation triggered during hover states: `transform: translateY(-8px); box-shadow: 0 20px 25px -5px rgba(30, 58, 95, 0.08), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(30, 58, 95, 0.04)`. Shadows carry an ultra-low navy tint (`#1E3A5F`) to maintain brand cohesion.
- **Floating Overlays:** Fixed UI elements (such as the bottom-right AI mascot widget) employ continuous floating vertical transitions coupled with an ambient shadow: `box-shadow: 0 12px 24px -4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.12)`.

## Shapes

The design system standardizes on a balanced rounded curvature:

- **Cards, Modals, and Media Embeds:** Structured with a consistent `12px` border radius (`rounded-xl` / `0.75rem`), delivering a friendly yet modern industrial feel.
- **Interactive Buttons & Badges:** Product actions and form inputs leverage `8px` to `12px` radii, while status indicators and high-priority action tags utilize fully rounded pill shapes (`9999px`) to maintain contrast against rectangular containers.
- **Image Clipping:** Product thumbnails inherit the card's top `12px` border radius while maintaining flat seams against card interior dividers.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Accent Button:** Background `#FFD700`, text `#1E3A5F` (Inter Semi-Bold), border-radius `12px` (or pill `9999px` for navigation CTAs). Hover state transitions to `#E6C200` with slight scale increase.
- **WhatsApp Direct Order Button:** Exclusively for transactional catalog purchasing. Full-width, background `#25D366`, text `#FFFFFF` (Semi-Bold), paired with an embedded WhatsApp vector icon. Hover state darkens to `#20BA5A`.
- **Secondary Navy Button:** Background `#1E3A5F`, text `#FFFFFF`. Hover elevates to Navy Dark `#13253D`.

### Cards (Product Catalog)
- **Structure:** Background `#FFFFFF`, border-radius `12px`, border `1px solid #F1F5F9`, ambient drop shadow Level 1.
- **States:** Transition smoothly on hover via `transition: transform 0.3s ease, box-shadow 0.3s ease; transform: translateY(-8px); box-shadow: Level 2`.
- **Anatomy:** 
  1. Top: Full-width fixed aspect ratio image (`object-fit: cover`).
  2. Content Body: Title in `#1E3A5F` (Inter Semi-Bold), Price formatted clearly (`Rp XX.XXX`), Dynamic Stock Badge.
  3. Action Footer: Padded base holding the full-width `#25D366` button.

### Badges & Stock Chips
- **Dynamic Stock Pill:** Border radius `9999px`, padding `4px 10px`, text `label-sm`. Low stock items display background `#FFD700` with text `#1E3A5F`; in-stock items display light green wash (`#E8FDF0`) with text `#15803D`.

### Navigation Bar
- **Sticky Behavior:** Fixed top (`z-index: 50`), transparent over hero banner top edge, transitioning to solid `#FFFFFF` with Level 1 elevation and Navy `#1E3A5F` branding upon scroll offset.

### AI Mascot Floating Widget
- **Placement & Styling:** Fixed position (`bottom-6 right-6`, `z-index: 100`). Features a friendly 2D mascot graphic (56px to 64px circular container) with ambient elevation.
- **Micro-interactions:** Subtle continuous floating keyframe animation (`translateY(0)` to `translateY(-6px)`).
- **Notification Trigger:** Absolute positioned alert badge on top-right corner in `#EF4444` featuring white numeric count (`1`).

### Footer
- **Structure:** Solid `#1E3A5F` background, text colors `#FFFFFF` and muted text `#94A3B8`. Integrated Google Maps embed styled with matching `12px` border radius and no harsh borders.
